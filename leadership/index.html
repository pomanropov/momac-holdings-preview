Leadership.

The group is led by its co-founders: Ryan McInerney, Chief Executive, and Mohammed Qadoumi, President.

Portrait of Ryan McInerney

Ryan McInerney

Chief Executive, co-founder

Ryan McInerney is Chief Executive of Momac Holdings and a co-founder of the group. He is also co-founder and Chief Executive of Gulf Bridge.

Mohammed Qadoumi

President, co-founder

Mohammed Qadoumi is President of Momac Holdings and a co-founder of the group. He is Managing Director of TREG Holdings and co-founder and President of Gulf Bridge. From 2010 to 2017 he was Executive Manager for Sales and Business Development across Top Real Estate Group and Top Expo, and he led the Canadian International Real Estate Marketplace in Toronto.
