The holding company behind five businesses.

Momac Holdings is the parent holding company of a group working in exhibitions, real estate, financial consulting and energy.

TopExpoExhibitions and conferences

TopExpo organises property and investment exhibitions, conferences and exhibitor pavilions. It was founded in Kuwait in 1998.

TREG Holdings markets international real estate to buyers in the Gulf. Between 2005 and 2019 the group marketed more than 120 projects in 12 countries.

Gulf Bridge works through two divisions. Private Markets introduces investment opportunities to investors whose mandates fit. Capital arranges finance for real estate owners, developers and sponsors.
