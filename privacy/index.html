What the contact form collects

The contact form asks for your name, company, email address, the subject of your enquiry and your message. You choose what to enter. Nothing is sent until you submit the form.

How it is handled

Submissions are delivered by Formspree, a form service, to the Momac Holdings enquiries mailbox. We use them to reply to you and, where your enquiry concerns one of the group companies, to pass it to that company. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Contact

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it removed, write to the enquiries address shown on the Contact page.
