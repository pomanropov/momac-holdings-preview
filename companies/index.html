TopExpo

Exhibitions and conferences

TopExpo organises property and investment exhibitions, conferences and exhibitor pavilions. It was founded in Kuwait in 1998.

TREG Holdings

Real estate

TREG Holdings markets international real estate to buyers in the Gulf. Between 2005 and 2019 the group marketed more than 120 projects in 12 countries.
